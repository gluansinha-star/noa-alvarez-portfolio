02About

Half designer, half engineer, fully obsessed with the seam.

Eleven years of product work, between the artboard and the editor.

The work is making a decision survive contact with a real interface.

I've spent more than a decade moving between Figma and a code editor. The prototype and the handoff come from the same person. Most of my work is for tools — developer platforms, research software, financial infrastructure — places where an interface has to carry a lot of meaning without raising its voice.

I care about type, motion, and the first ninety seconds of a product. I care less about trends. I studied interaction design in Providence, shipped my first product in Lagos, and now work independently from Brooklyn with a small group of collaborators I trust.
